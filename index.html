How Sprout works

Three steps to a calmer household.

1⏱️

Set the limits

You choose a daily or weekly allowance and how gently points phase out after it — different rules for school days and weekends.

2⭐

Kids earn points

Stay under the limit and keep the full daily points. Go over and they fade out slowly — never a hard punishment.

3🎁

Redeem rewards

Kids spend points in a shop you build: extra screen time, a movie night, a snack, or a big goal like a game console.
